# Agent avatars

The gem an agent session is drawn as by its role, as zirc-proto's `AGENT_AVATARS` lists them:
the cut stone of zirc's logo with a face, wearing or holding what its role works with.
They're zirc's own drawings, under zirc's own licence: flat colour on a 48 by 48 grid, on
nothing, for apps to put on a session's colour in a rounded square. The stone is white that
lets that colour through.

Made by zirc-theme-art (`src/avatars.rs`): `bazel run //crates/zirc-theme-art`.
