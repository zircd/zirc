# Coworker characters

The twelve characters a coworker is drawn as, by the ids zirc-proto's `characters::CHARACTERS`
lists: `<id>-bust-<side>.png`, a head and shoulders that fills a coworker's rounded square edge
to edge, and `<id>-full-<width>.png`, the whole figure, for the office's cards and the hiring
gallery. Transparent, for apps to draw on the coworker's colour. zirc's own, under zirc's
licence.

Made by zirc-theme-art (`src/characters.rs`) from the stills in
`crates/zirc-theme-art/characters`: `bazel run //crates/zirc-theme-art`.
